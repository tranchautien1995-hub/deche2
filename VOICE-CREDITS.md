# Vietnamese voice credits

Original short Vietnamese dialogue for the mobile controls is rendered by [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) using the [vais1000 medium model from the Piper voices project](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/v1.0.0/vi/vi_VN/vais1000/medium).

The voice model uses the VAIS 1000 Vietnamese Speech Synthesis Corpus, as recorded in its [model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/v1.0.0/vi/vi_VN/vais1000/medium/MODEL_CARD). The corpus is licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The packaged responses use synthesized speech from original text. Pitch is adjusted for soldiers in the game.
